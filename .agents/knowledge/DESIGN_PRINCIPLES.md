# DESIGN PRINCIPLES & ANTI-AI AESTHETIC RULES (Layer 3 Memory)

> **Governance Purpose:** Concrete, actionable design principles and aesthetic boundaries for the POSTLAIN creative engineering system.

---

## 1. Concrete Design Principles for POSTLAIN

1. **Hierarchy Before Decoration:** Typography, spatial rhythm, and proportion must establish complete visual and narrative hierarchy before any border, background tint, or motion effect is introduced.
2. **Authenticity Over Futurism:** Visual elements must reflect Ngô Phúc's genuine dual reality: high-stress operational leadership (culinary, retail, studio) and refined sonic production. Never default to generic sci-fi or cyberpunk aesthetics.
3. **Restraint as High Craft:** The benchmark for Awwwards-level execution is editorial discipline and deliberate white space, not how many visual tricks are packed into the viewport.
4. **Natural Physics & Native Respect:** Scroll physics, pointer interactions, and text selection must respect the operating system's native standards. Custom interactions must enhance, not hijack, browser fundamentals.
5. **Semantic Accentuation:** Accent colors (e.g. lime or amber) must be reserved for interactive signals, primary focal points, or verified status, never sprayed as ambient background noise.
6. **Legibility & Typographic Contrast:** Display typography (Montserrat / Cormorant Garamond) must pair with high-legibility body type (Plus Jakarta Sans) with line lengths strictly constrained between `45ch` and `75ch` and minimum contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AA (4.5:1).
7. **Narrative-Driven Motion:** Animations must have a clear physical or narrative purpose (e.g., spatial continuity, revealing relationships, state transitions). Transitions must remain within `200ms - 400ms` with GPU-accelerated properties (`transform`, `opacity`).
8. **Real Music Over Synthetic Bleeps:** Audio integration must showcase actual musical production and curated sonic craft (Hidden Music), rather than procedural click sound effects.
9. **Zero Fake Chrome:** Every number, timestamp, coordinate, or status indicator must be factual. Banish simulated metrics, fake FPS counters, and mock server latency widgets.
10. **Mobile-First Ergonomics:** Touch targets must be at least `44x44px`. Layouts must reflow gracefully across small viewports without horizontal scrolling or squashed modal boxes.
11. **Opt-In Sensory Experiences:** Sound, canvas effects, and heavy interactive layers must default to an unobtrusive baseline with clear, user-controlled activation.
12. **Bilingual Elegance:** Vietnamese typography must be treated with equal typographical respect, avoiding font mismatches, clipped diacritics, or clumsy machine-translated phrasing.

## 2. Anti-AI Aesthetic Rules (Banned Patterns)

| Banned AI Pattern | Why It Is Prohibited | Replacement Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monolithic Pill Buttons & Capsules Everywhere** | Feels like an AI design template or component kit. | Clean typographic buttons, underline anchors, and structured editorial bounds. |
| **Persistent Cyan/Purple/Lime Neon Glows** | Screams generic "AI crypto/web3 startup". | Deep charcoal/slate surfaces, warm muted neutrals, and crisp monochromatic contrast. |
| **Excessive Glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-2xl`)** | Creates muddy legibility and high GPU compositing overhead. | Solid, deliberate surface colors with subtle 1px structural dividers. |
| **Fake Terminal / Sci-Fi HUD Badges (`ACT 01 // OVERTURE`)** | Pretends a human portfolio is a spaceship console. | Editorial section numbers, chapter headers, or clean thematic titles. |
| **Decorative 3D Particles / Wireframe Spheres** | Pure visual filler with zero storytelling value. | High-quality imagery, typography-driven layouts, and meaningful interactive visualizers. |
| **Custom Crosshair Cursor Overrides** | Degrades user control, latency, and accessibility. | Native OS pointer with subtle CSS hover transitions. |
| **100vh Rigid Viewport Interception** | Breaks scroll wheel, trackpad gestures, and zoom. | Fluid document flow with sticky editorial pinpoints or smooth section snaps. |
| **All-Caps Monospace Overload** | Causes cognitive fatigue and diminishes readability. | Standard sentence/title case with selective tabular numbers for dates/stats. |
